Got questions?

How does {{ brand.app_name }} work?

Paste a video link or upload a file. The app gets a word-level transcript, picks the most shareable moments, crops them to the format you choose while following the speaker, adds animated captions and renders each clip. You can then edit any clip and re-render it.

What types of videos can I upload?

Most video and audio files (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, MP3, WAV and more), and links from YouTube and many other video sites. Talking-head content such as podcasts, interviews, streams and lectures works best. Upload size and length limits are shown on your dashboard.

Which languages are supported?

Transcription works in 99 languages and can detect the language automatically. Captions can also be translated to English.

Can I edit the captions and clips?

Yes. Change a clip's start and end, title, hashtags and caption style, add your own clips from the transcript, and download captions as SRT, WebVTT or ASS to edit them anywhere.

Is {{ brand.app_name }} free to use?

Creating an account is free. Clips have no watermarks. During the beta each account has fair-use limits on videos per day and storage, shown on your dashboard.

Where are my videos stored?

On the server that runs {{ brand.app_name }}. Your projects are private to your account, and you can download or delete them at any time.
