Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what {{ brand.app_name }} ("we", "the service") collects, why, and how you control it. {{ brand.app_name }} turns long videos into short clips and, if you choose, posts those clips to the TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts you connect.

What we collect

  • Your account: your username and a securely hashed password. We never store your password in readable form.
  • Videos you provide: files you upload or links you paste, the clips made from them, their transcripts, titles and hashtags.
  • Connected social accounts: when you connect a TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account, the platform gives us an access key for that account, plus its public profile name, username and profile picture. We never see or store your password for those platforms.
  • Posting history: which clips you posted to which account, when, and the link to the published post.
  • Technical logs: error reports needed to keep the service running.

How we use it

  • To make your clips and show them only to you.
  • To post a clip to a connected account only when you ask us to, with the caption and settings you choose. We do not post, like, comment, follow or message on your behalf in any other way.
  • To read your connected account's basic profile so you can tell your accounts apart.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the platforms you post to and the storage provider described below.

How we protect it

Access keys for connected accounts are encrypted before they are stored. Your videos and clips are private to your account. Connections to the service use HTTPS.

  • Connected account keys are kept until you disconnect the account or delete your {{ brand.app_name }} account.
  • Posting history is kept until you delete your {{ brand.app_name }} account.

Your choices

  • Disconnect an account at any time in Settings, under Connected accounts. Its keys are deleted at once.
  • You can also remove {{ brand.app_name }}'s access from the platform itself: in TikTok's settings under security and permissions, in Instagram's settings under apps and websites, or in your Google account's third-party access page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Delete projects and clips yourself at any time, or ask us to delete your whole account and everything in it.

Third-party services

  • TikTok, Instagram (Meta) and YouTube (Google): clips you post are sent to them and are then covered by their own policies. {{ brand.app_name }} uses the YouTube API Services; by connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's use of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloud storage: clips may be stored with Cloudflare R2, a private storage service, so they can be played and downloaded.

Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

The service is not intended for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age to use TikTok, Instagram or YouTube in their country.
